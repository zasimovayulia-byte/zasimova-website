About the Artist

Biography

Yuliia Zasimova is an acclaimed Ukrainian soprano renowned for her brilliant vocal technique, dramatic depth, and captivating stage presence across leading European opera houses.

Her repertoire spans classical bel canto masterpieces, Mozartean roles, and modern dramatic compositions. She regularly collaborates with prominent conductors and symphony orchestras.
