Biography & Highlights

Ukrainian soprano Yuliia Zasimova has quickly established herself as one of the most compelling young voices on European opera stages. Celebrated for her brilliant coloratura technique, rich lyric warmth, and arresting dramatic presence, she effortlessly spans repertoire from Mozart and Bel Canto to 20th-century opera.

Her formative years at the prestigious International Opera Studio of Opernhaus Zürich laid the cornerstone for her international career, enabling her to collaborate with world-renowned conductors and directors in major theatrical productions across Switzerland, Germany, France, and Ukraine.
